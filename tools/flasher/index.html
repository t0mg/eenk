⚠️ Safety Warning
⚠️ CRITICAL WARNING — READ BEFORE PROCEEDING

The device MUST be USB unlocked before proceeding. Failure to USB unlock prior to flashing WILL BRICK THE DEVICE.

Technical reason: The eenk firmware replaces the stock partition table, allocating roughly half of the device's 16 MB flash as read-only storage for loading and running Ink stories. The USB unlock procedure is required to allow overwriting the partition table. Flashing without this step will result in a device that cannot boot.

Before you continue, ensure you have completed the USB unlock procedure described in the eenky documentation. If you are reinstalling or updating firmware that was already unlocked, you may proceed.
